## Generated mechanic

Choose two hovering cargo cubes and exchange just their height, width-position, or depth-position. Both cubes slide simultaneously along that world axis; their other coordinates stay unchanged. Helping one cube always relocates its partner, and both routes must be clear.

## Novelty and playtesting

A useful comparison is The Swapper, which creates clones and transfers consciousness between bodies. Crosshaul instead moves two existing objects by exchanging one coordinate, coupling their physical routes. [The Swapper's official description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/231160/The_Swapper/). Among the supplied concepts, Color Custody exchanges behavioral ownership; Crosshaul exchanges spatial components. Perspective Pocket changes placement through silhouette constraints; Crosshaul is independent of camera perspective and preserves coordinate inventories. This appears distinct from the supplied mechanics, but worldwide originality and fun cannot be certified.

The rule could feel like abstract coordinate sorting, while occlusion could make failures seem arbitrary. Keep chambers small, preview both complete routes, distinguish axes with labels and arrow shapes, and test whether obstacles create interesting partner choices rather than repeated corrective swaps.

### 1. tooling (medium)

Observed: xcapture.py failed at XOpenDisplay. Direct Xvfb startup revealed denied socket binding; a Unix-socket probe returned Operation not permitted.

Reproduce: Run tools/xcapture.py against the crosshaul binary in this sandbox.

Workaround: Completed headless tests and package checks. Native review criteria and scripted gameplay are provided for a display-enabled host.

Proposed engine improvement: Preflight display readiness and retain Xvfb stderr so socket restrictions produce a clear diagnostic.

### 2. tooling (low)

Observed: Installer preparation against the Linux package reported a title/executable identity mismatch instead of identifying the missing Windows package.

Reproduce: Package on Linux, then run python3 scripts/ship.py installer prepare.

Workaround: Prepare the installer after packaging on Windows.

Proposed engine improvement: Detect a host-package versus Windows-package mismatch and report that Windows packaging is required.

### 3. rendering (low)

Observed: Cargo labels, coordinates and docking status jump to destinations while meshes are still sliding. The frame-30 capture shows 'OK' and all cargo docked before visual arrival. Meshes interpolate at src/main.rs:384; labels use endpoint positions at :466.

Reproduce: Execute the first height exchange and inspect its intermediate frames.

Workaround: Wait for the short slide to finish; completion correctly waits for motion to end.

Proposed engine improvement: Anchor labels to the interpolated cargo positions and defer arrival indicators until travel completes.

### 4. audio (low)

Observed: Source inspection shows ordinary slide arrivals miss their docking click. Endpoint dock counts update when motion starts, but playback requires motion to have ended; previous_docked is already updated by then (src/main.rs:359). Final success has a separate cue.

Reproduce: Trace an exchange that increases docked cargo: the count rises during motion, then remains unchanged at arrival.

Workaround: Visual docking indicators and the final success cue remain available.

Proposed engine improvement: Compare docking counts across the completed motion or retain the pre-slide count until arrival.

### 5. audio (medium)

Observed: Engine/dependency friction: the isolated native smoke log records an ALSA backend thread panic while setting its sample rate. Rendering and chamber completion continued, so the successful graphical smoke does not establish working audio.

Reproduce: Run the packaged native smoke on the same host audio configuration and inspect stderr for backend initialization failure.

Workaround: Use the completed captures for graphical review. No audio workaround was verified on this host; application muting does not prevent Macroquad backend initialization.

Proposed engine improvement: Handle unsupported audio-device configurations without a thread panic and expose backend failure separately from graphical smoke success.
