## Generated mechanic

Moving a selected crate also carries its immediate diagonal neighbors. Those passengers do not recruit their own neighbors. Choosing the driver changes which part of an arrangement moves: a diagonal three-crate chain moves entirely when driven from its middle, but splits when driven from an end.

## Novelty and playtesting

The closest comparison is [Jelly Is Sticky](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1492620/Jelly_Is_Sticky/), which builds puzzles around sticking and deforming blocks. Corner Clutch specifically uses driver-dependent, nonrecursive diagonal pickup. Compared with prior Gap Flip, movement is a local one-cell translation rather than corridor reflection; compared with Odd Matter, occupied cells never cancel. These are concrete differences, not certification of worldwide originality or fun.

Diagonal pickup may feel arbitrary, and direct selection may make ordinary positioning too easy. A diagonal pair needs a third crate to separate its members; once any diagonal bond exists, at least one bond always remains somewhere. Goals must respect that invariant. Reject repetitive shuffling rooms instead of adding gadgets to disguise shallow play.
