## Generated mechanic

Flip a token within its current clear corridor, exchanging the empty space on its two sides. One empty cell left and four right becomes four left and one right. Walls and other tokens bound the corridor, so moving one token changes where another token's next flip lands.

## Novelty and playtesting

The closest comparison is Ricochet Robots, where walls and other robots determine stopping positions ([publisher description](https://www.riograndegames.com/games/ricochet-robots/)). Here a token reflects within its available corridor instead of sliding to an obstacle: interior clearance determines its landing, and repeating a flip restores its position while boundaries remain unchanged. Among the supplied prior mechanics, Inside-Out Key also emphasizes empty space, but swaps container contents with surrounding negative space; Gap Flip has no containers or contents exchange. None of the supplied mechanics uses this corridor-reflection rule, and the slug is absent from the supplied list. Worldwide originality and fun remain unverified.

Reflection may initially feel like arbitrary teleportation, and badly chosen rooms may reduce to repetitive symmetry toggles. Keep gap bands and destination previews visible; retain only rooms where players discover and use changing token boundaries.
