# Afterpull: AI development feedback

Run: `dc78009a`.
Project: `games/afterpull`. State: published.

## Generated mechanic

Every square you leave briefly becomes a vacuum: it pulls the nearest visible crate in each cardinal direction one tile toward your empty footprint. Your new position blocks one direction. Walking therefore rearranges several distant crates at once, even when you only wanted to cross the room.

## Novelty and playtesting

The closest comparison is magnetic Sokoban: [NABOKOS](https://pixelgrapher.itch.io/nabokos) explicitly uses a magnet to pull boxes onto buttons. Afterpull's proposed distinction is that attraction originates from the square just vacated, automatically affects several unobstructed directions, and supports intentional destination-conflict jams. None of the supplied prior mechanics uses this departure-triggered spatial rule. This comparison does not certify worldwide originality or fun.

Walking may feel like unpredictable collateral damage, or levels may reduce to routine single-crate pulling. The prototype must show that previews make outcomes legible and that players deliberately use shielding, simultaneous motion and jams. If those interactions rarely matter, the central rule needs reconsideration before expansion.
