# Rift Delver status

Implemented: spatial 3D movement and aiming, collision-consistent cover, six enemy types, three weapons, three biome layouts, seeded upgrade drafts, twelve stackable circuits, extraction risk, four permanent workshop tracks, weapon unlocks, lifetime kill contracts, automatic exact snapshots, keyboard/mouse/controller support, generated native identity resources, sound effects and ambient music.

Validation: deterministic replay, exact save continuation, damaged-save rejection, combat, upgrades, workshop economy, extraction, insurance, boss spawning, weapon unlocks and a 50-minute simulated endurance session. Development captures of combat, workshop, circuit draft and Warden encounter are visually reviewed. Final package and platform evidence are recorded in ship.json and the linked CI runs.

Limits: automated agents and virtual input are not human playtest feedback. Physical controller hardware and audible speaker output require a player. This first release uses procedural low-poly art and three repeated environment layouts; variety comes from combat combinations, increasing depths, weapon choice and circuit builds.
