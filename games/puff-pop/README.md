# Puff Pop

A 2D hot-seat game for two people sharing one controller. Pip and Poppy are two pufferfish diving through a reef. Hold UP (stick, D-pad or W) to puff and float, release to sink, hold DOWN to squeeze small. Thread coral gaps, grab pearls in a chain for a multiplier, and pop bubble walls while fully puffed. Three dives each, then the controller passes. Lifetime pearls unlock hats.

Hold UP to inflate, DOWN to squeeze, A/Space confirms, N music, M sound, K save, L load, R restart (records and hats survive restart).

Progress is saved automatically about once a second and restored on launch; K/L make a manual checkpoint. Music is generated from `assets/audio-source/music.json` (engine `be2-tools audio render`) and shipped as `assets/audio/music`.
