# Puff Pop

A single-player 2D game. Pip the pufferfish dives through an endless reef. Hold UP (stick, D-pad or W) to puff and float, release to sink, hold DOWN to squeeze small. Thread coral gaps, grab pearls in a chain for a multiplier, and pop bubble walls while fully puffed. Three hearts per dive; lifetime pearls unlock hats for Pip.

Hold UP to inflate, DOWN to squeeze, A/Space starts and retries, N music, M sound, K save, L load, R restart (records and hats survive restart).

Progress is saved automatically about once a second and restored on launch; K/L make a manual checkpoint. Music is generated from `assets/audio-source/music.json` (engine `be2-tools audio render`) and shipped as `assets/audio/music`.
