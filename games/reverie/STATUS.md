# Reverie implementation

Implemented: first-person engine controller and collision; seeded endless branching galleries; eight emotional themes; six living works per gallery; artwork names, fictional artists and stories; five frames; recursively traversable paintings; exact return poses; one key per gallery, including keys two or three paintings deep and sculpture keys; two locked doors and backtracking; collected-key and room counters; pause/map/settings; eight composed scores with room crossfades and adaptive depth; independent footsteps toggle; keyboard, mouse and controller input; engine-owned snapshots and native packaging.

Validation evidence is retained under `.blue-check`. Generated audio is checked and recreated from `assets/score` by `scripts/compose.py`. Only the current geometry is retained. Physical audio hardware and a human's assessment of the composition are separate from automated audio asset/playback checks.
